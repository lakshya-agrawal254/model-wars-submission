## Observation limits

The actor sees only local current/predicted ball positions, distance, speed, availability and timing. It has no opponent position or field/goal-relative heading. A180degree rotation of car, ball and their histories while keeping the team fixed can preserve these local observations but reverse which global goal should be attacked. Therefore reliable own-goal/enemy-goal identification is not generally possible under the contract, even with recurrent history. History can detect ball-trajectory changes, not directly track opponents or uniquely identify who caused them. True global coordinates/team signs remain solely in reward and evaluation code.
