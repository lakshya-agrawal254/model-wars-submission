## Pilot audit and a necessary optimizer correction

The first two batches completed131072 PPO-eligible decisions plus12153 deterministic prefix decisions,8 actual actor updates in175.76seconds. They reached two brief slow collisions and five circling proxies, but no sustained-stationary reward. The transient collision trigger fired after3.5seconds of kickoff, with speed recovery approximately0.4seconds later: this did not reproduce the prolonged pinned state. All149 earlier checkpoint checksums and exact recurrent/likelihood/save-load checks passed.

Reducing exploration also exposed a trust-region bug: the inherited PPO guard measured KL before the optimizer step and only stopped after another step. The first batch's next-pass estimated KL was0.18147. This is not an action-density mismatch; stored behavior likelihoods reproduce correctly. It is an ineffective update-size safeguard, especially at smaller steering variance. The pilot is preserved and not eligible for continuation from its weights.

The new training-only bounded_actor_step measures the ACTUAL post-proposal distribution on complete recorded recurrent histories: categorical KL plus Chase-probability-weighted latent-Gaussian KL. It accepts only meanKL<=0.01 and maximum trajectory meanKL<=0.025; otherwise both weights and every Adam moment/step/LR are restored and a smaller step is tried. Regression tests on copied real-state histories verified exact rollback for empty and populated Adam, and reduced a0.20797 proposal KL to0.0032526 before acceptance. The test actor is discarded. Proposal attempts and accepted optimizer updates are reported separately.
